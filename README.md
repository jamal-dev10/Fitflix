# FitFlex Gym & High-Performance Fitness Platform

A complete, production-grade fitness web application built with **React 18**, **TypeScript**, **Vite 6**, and **Tailwind CSS**.

## Complete Feature Suite

1. **Top Bar Navigation**:
   - Compliant with the 3-zone Top Bar Contract.
   - Quick section links, fast-access tools for the **Gym Floor Interval Timer** and **Digital Member Pass**, and primary CTA: **Book Free Trial**.

2. **Hero & Atmospheric Showcase**:
   - Background gym footage demonstration (`livecoaching.mp4`).
   - Quantified proof strip (15+ Certified Coaches, 40+ Weekly Classes, 98% Goal Success Rate, 100% Tailored Macro Nutrition).
   - Instant 1-day pass claim and program explorer.

3. **Core Services ("Experience. Expertise. Excellence.")**:
   - Signature yellow indicator line expansion, image zoom, and quick navigation into Programs, Personal Training, and Nutrition.

4. **Interactive Workout Program Catalog**:
   - Dynamic category filtering (All, Strength, Yoga, Cardio & HIIT, Functional).
   - Intensity & duration tags, original dumbbell/yoga/muscle icons.
   - **View Routine Modal**: Full exercise breakdowns with recommended sets, reps, and coaching cues.

5. **Facility & Equipment Showcase**:
   - Virtual tour across 3 specialized zones: Olympic Barbell & Power Racks, Functional Sled Turf, and Infrared Sauna & Cold Plunge.

6. **Personal Training & Virtual Live Coaching**:
   - Interactive toggle between In-Gym 1-on-1 and Virtual Live Coaching.
   - Video player with play/pause and mute/unmute controls.
   - Certified coach bios with specialties and direct consultation booking.

7. **Precision Nutrition Science**:
   - Interactive 3 pillars (Personalized Meal Plans, Nutritional Coaching, Recipe Database).
   - High-protein chef recipes with macro breakdowns (Calories, Protein, Carbs, Fat) and expandable preparation instructions.

8. **Weekly Class Schedule & Spot Reservations**:
   - 7-day schedule (Monday to Sunday) with live capacity meters and reservation toggle with instant confirmation.

9. **Verified Client Transformations & Proof**:
   - Concrete, quantified outcomes (e.g. -16kg fat / +35kg squat PR, -9% body fat, mobility rehabilitation) with real coach attributions.

10. **Membership Pricing & Instant Enrollment**:
    - Monthly vs Annual billing toggle with 25% savings calculation.
    - 3 tiers: Day/Starter Pass, Black Card Elite (Flagship), and Pro Performance + PT.
    - Interactive enrollment modal that generates a digital pass upon completion.

11. **Digital Member Pass Generator**:
    - Verifiable digital membership card with dynamic Member Name, Tier, Pass ID Token, barcode, and print/save capability.

12. **Gym Floor Interval Timer & Stopwatch**:
    - Built-in HIIT / Tabata / Rest timer with Web Audio API sound buzzer.
    - Presets for Tabata (20/10s), HIIT (45/15s), and Heavy Lifting Rest (60s).
    - Customizable work, rest, and round parameters.

13. **Biometric Calculator**:
    - Real-time BMI, BMR, TDEE, and macronutrient targets (Protein, Carbs, Fats) calculation supporting Metric and Imperial units.

14. **About FitFlex & Gym Heritage**:
    - Philosophy statement, values, and original graphic assets (`Noexcuses-01.png`, `morocco.svg`).

15. **Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)**:
    - Categorized accordion answering common membership, class, facility, and nutrition questions.

16. **Validated Inquiry Form**:
    - Contact form with client-side validation and immediate submission confirmation.
